No.06

Straight answers

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Walk in if a chair's free, but booking is the only way to be sure. The online diary shows every free slot, and you get a text the day before.

Do you take a deposit?

Not for a cut. The Full Works and group bookings take a £10 deposit per person when you book, taken off the price on the day.

What if I need to cancel?

Move or cancel free up to 24 hours before with the link in your text. Miss two bookings without telling us and we'll ask for a deposit next time.

Do you colour or blend greys?

Yes, grey blending for hair and beard. It needs a patch test at least 48 hours before; it takes five minutes and it's free.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes, £16 for under-12s, with a parent or carer in the shop. Saturday mornings go fast.

Can you do a groom's party?

Yes. We open early for wedding mornings, with cuts and shaves for up to six. Send us the date below and we'll put a plan together.
